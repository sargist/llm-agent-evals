title: Lost or stolen card
keywords: [lost, stolen, missing, block, freeze, replacement, replace, card]
---
If your card is lost or stolen, block it straight away in the app (Cards > Block card) or ask the virtual assistant. Blocking takes effect immediately. A blocked card cannot be unblocked online.

When you block a card as lost or stolen, a replacement card is ordered automatically and delivered within 5-7 business days. Replacing a debit card costs 5 EUR. Replacing a credit card is free.

If you see payments you did not make, also open a dispute (see "Disputing a card transaction").
