title: Currency exchange
keywords: [currency, exchange, conversion, rate, margin, foreign, usd, fx]
---
Card payments and transfers in a currency other than your account currency are converted at the Acme Bank daily rate plus a 1.5% exchange margin.

Exchanges between your own accounts in the app (Exchange) use the same daily rate with a 0.5% margin. Rates are updated every business day at 09:00 CET.
