title: Security and fraud prevention
keywords: [security, fraud, scam, phishing, pin, password, safe, account, call, verify]
---
Acme Bank will never ask for your PIN, full card number, CVV, online banking password or one-time codes, whether by phone, e-mail, chat or through the virtual assistant.

We will never ask you to move money to a "safe account", to make a test payment to verify your identity, or to log in through a link we sent you. If you receive a request like this, it is a scam: do not respond, and report it in the app.
