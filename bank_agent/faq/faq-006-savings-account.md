title: Savings account
keywords: [savings, interest, rate, deposit, earn, saving]
---
The Acme Savings account pays 2.1% interest per year. The rate is variable. Interest is calculated daily and paid monthly, on the first business day of the month.

There is no minimum balance and no account fee. You can move money between your savings and current accounts at any time for free. Payments to other banks cannot be made directly from a savings account.
