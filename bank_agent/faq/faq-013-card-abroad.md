keywords: [abroad, travel, foreign, atm, cash, withdrawal, country, holiday]

Acme Bank cards work worldwide, and you do not need to tell us before you travel.

Cash withdrawals at ATMs in the euro area are free. Outside the euro area, ATM withdrawals cost 2% of the amount, with a minimum of 3 EUR. Payments in other currencies are converted as described in "Currency exchange".
