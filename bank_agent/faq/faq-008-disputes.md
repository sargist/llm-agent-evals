title: Disputing a card transaction
keywords: [dispute, chargeback, unrecognised, unknown, unauthorized, wrong, payment, transaction]
---
If you do not recognise a card transaction, contact support within 60 days of the transaction date. We investigate and, where the card scheme rules allow, start a chargeback with the merchant. An investigation usually takes up to 45 days.

If you think your card details were stolen, block the card first.
